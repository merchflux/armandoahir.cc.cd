The 3 Materials to Always Avoid

1. Jelly and PVC dildos — porous, frequently loaded with phthalates, they smell strongly, leach oils and cannot be cleaned properly. Sold cheaply in novelty stores; never worth the health risk.

2. TPE / TPR "real feel" dildos — softer than silicone but porous, so they trap bacteria. They also react badly with silicone lube and degrade over time.

3. Cyberskin / UR3 dildos — marketed as ultra-realistic skin, but porous and high-maintenance. They peel, stain and cannot be sterilized. Choose dual-density silicone instead.

How to Clean and Care for Body-Safe Dildos

  1. After every use: wash with warm water and mild unscented soap or toy cleaner.
  2. Deep sanitizing: boil silicone, glass and steel toys for 3–5 minutes, or wipe with a 10% bleach solution and rinse.
  3. Never boil: toys with motors, or any porous material.
  4. Store separately: silicone toys can absorb oils and react with other materials — keep each toy in its own pouch.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the safest material for a dildo?

Platinum-cured silicone, borosilicate glass and surgical stainless steel are the safest dildo materials. All three are non-porous, non-toxic, phthalate-free and can be fully sterilized, so they will not harbor bacteria.

Is silicone or glass better for a dildo?

Silicone is soft, flexible and warms to body temperature, which makes it more comfortable and realistic. Glass is ultra-smooth, firm and great for temperature play, and it works with any lubricant. Both are body-safe — choose based on the feel you want.

Are jelly dildos safe?

No. Jelly, PVC and TPE are porous materials that can contain phthalates, leach oils and harbor bacteria. They cannot be fully sterilized and can even degrade over time. Never use them internally — stick to silicone, glass or steel.

How do you clean a silicone or glass dildo?

Wash with warm water and mild unscented soap or a dedicated toy cleaner after every use. For deep cleaning, boil pure silicone, glass and steel toys for 3-5 minutes or wipe with a 10% bleach solution. Never boil toys that contain motors.

Can I use silicone lubricant with a silicone dildo?

No — silicone lubricant can break down silicone toys over time. Use water-based or hybrid lube with silicone dildos. Glass and steel dildos are safe with water-based, silicone or hybrid lubricants.
